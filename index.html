Eight centuries of silence

The shattering

800 years ago

The war of the first god's sons

Chronos's twelve princes rose against the demon lords who plagued the young world. One by one the brothers fell, until their line went out of existence — leaving only three demon lords standing: Asmodeus, Leviathan, and Azazel.

The first Luminary

Light against the abyss

When humanity inherited the gods' blood, the Luminaries were born — wielders of unrivaled light. The first of them stood against the demon lords alone. It was not enough. He fell, and the war seemed lost.

The forging

Escanor, blade of heaven

Every mage in the world set aside their rivalries to forge a single sword. Escanor turned the tide, driving the demon lords' forces back and sealing the abyss from the human realm.

500 years of peace

A world that forgot to be afraid

Generations lived and died without ever seeing a demon. The seal held. The Luminaries became legend. And in the quiet, something began to stir among those who still hungered for the old, dark power.

Now

The Abyssm rise

A cult of devil-touched mages works to shatter the seal and return Asmodeus to the throne he lost. The kingdom needs a new Luminary — before the abyss remembers how to open.

Forged by every mage alive

Escanor, the blade of heaven

No single hero could stop the demon lords. It took the whole world, working as one, to forge the sword that could. Escanor drove the abyss shut and has waited five centuries for a hand worthy of the Luminaries who came before.

Now the seal it made is failing — and the blade is choosing again.

The seal is failing

The Abyssm

Devil-touched mages who abandoned the old wards for forbidden power, the Abyssm have spent years hunting fragments of the ritual that bound Asmodeus. Every fragment they recover widens the crack. Humanity's mages hold the line where the first Luminary once stood alone — this time, they don't intend to fall the same way.
